## 🏛️ Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) Registration Guide

When registering your company via the MCA **SPICe+ Part A (Name Reservation)** form:

1. **The Unique Coined Name Advantage**:
   - The word **"Baagupadu"** is an invented/cultural coined word not found in standard English dictionaries.
   - This means **it has an almost 100% first-time approval rate** because there are no conflicting corporate names on the MCA registry.
2. **Activity Keyword Requirement**:
   - Indian company law requires a word indicating your business activity (e.g. `AI`, `Labs`, `Cognitive`, `Companions`, `Systems`).
   - Suffixes like **AI**, **Labs**, or **Companions** satisfy this statutory requirement immediately.
3. **Trademark (IP India - Class 42 & Class 9)**:
   - File the trademark for **Baagupadu** under **Class 42** (Software as a Service / AI services) and **Class 9** (Downloadable software/AI models).

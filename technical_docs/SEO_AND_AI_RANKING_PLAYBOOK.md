### Step 2: Verify in Google Search Console (Mandatory for Google #1)
Google does not index brand-new domains immediately unless requested:

1. Open **[Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)** and sign in with your Google account.
2. Under **Select property type**, choose **Domain** (left box).
3. Enter `baagupadu.com` and click **Continue**.
4. Google will display a **DNS TXT record** string (e.g., `google-site-verification=abc123xyz...`).
5. Open your **Hostinger hPanel** (`hpanel.hostinger.com`) → **Domains** → `baagupadu.com` → **DNS / Nameservers**.
6. Add the record:
   - **Type**: `TXT`
   - **Name / Host**: `@`
   - **TXT Value**: *(Paste the google-site-verification code from Google)*
   - **TTL**: `300` (or default)
   - Click **Add Record**.
7. Return to Google Search Console and click **Verify**.
8. Once verified:
   - On the left sidebar, click **Sitemaps**.
   - Under *Add a new sitemap*, type `sitemap.xml` and click **Submit**.
   - In the top search bar (*"Inspect any URL in 'baagupadu.com'"*), paste `https://baagupadu.com` and press Enter.
   - Click **Request Indexing**. This places your site in Google's priority crawl queue (indexes within 24–48 hours).

### Step 3: Verify in Bing Webmaster Tools (Powers ChatGPT & Copilot)

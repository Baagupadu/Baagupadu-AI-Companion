## 1. Editorial Philosophy & Standards

Baagupadu’s organic acquisition strategy strictly rejects low-quality generative "SEO blog spam." In accordance with Google's helpful content systems and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) standards, every piece of published content must deliver:
1. **First-Hand Engineering Experience:** Written from the authentic perspective of building Baagupadu's multi-agent cognitive engine, FastAPI backend, pgvector retrieval, and frontend scrollytelling.
2. **Empirical Evidence & Code Artifacts:** Incorporate real architectural snippets, benchmark data, and concrete mathematical formulas (e.g. RRF, ROUGE-L).
3. **No Fictional Case Studies:** Customer stories must reflect genuine pilot evaluations, anonymous research surveys, or transparent developmental benchmarks.
4. **Editorial Integrity:** No article is published live without explicit founder review and approval.

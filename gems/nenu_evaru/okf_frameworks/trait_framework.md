## Inference Rules

### Pattern Matching

#### Minimum Indicators For Confidence
**High:** 3

**Moderate:** 2

**Low:** 1


#### Life Stage Weighting
**Childhood:** 0.2

**Teenage:** 0.3

**Adult:** 0.5


#### Recency Weighting
**Description:** Recent patterns (Adult) weigh more heavily than past patterns (Childhood)

**Formula:** Confidence = (Childhood_indicators × 0.2) + (Teenage_indicators × 0.3) + (Adult_indicators × 0.5)


#### Fallback Rules
**Description:** If the user hasn't completed a life stage, confidence levels adjust accordingly.


##### Rules
- If only Childhood is complete: Max confidence = Medium
- If Childhood + Teenage are complete: Max confidence = High (for consistent patterns)
- If Adult is not complete: Use weighted score with higher emphasis on Teenage + Childhood

### Confidence Levels

#### High
**Criteria:** Pattern appears in 2+ life stages with 3+ indicators each

**Presentation:** You consistently show [trait]


#### Moderate
**Criteria:** Pattern appears in 2+ life stages with limited evidence OR 1 life stage with strong evidence

**Presentation:** I notice a pattern of [trait]


#### Low
**Criteria:** Pattern appears in 1 life stage with limited evidence

**Presentation:** I wonder if you might be [trait]
